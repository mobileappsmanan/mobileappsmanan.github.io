Help and contact

Support

Paws Above is built around a small set of controls and quick runs. These notes cover the most common questions.

Does Paws Above require an account?

No. The game works without registration or sign-in.

How do I control the jump?

Swipe up for a higher jump and swipe down for a shallower jump. The goal is to land on the next candle platform.

What are rescues?

A run begins with three bed rescues. A rescue returns the cat safely after a missed landing. When they are gone, an optional rewarded continue may be offered.

Where are my high scores stored?

High scores, tutorial completion and SFX settings are stored locally on the Android device.

Can I control the audio?

The Settings screen includes a sound-effects control. Paws Above does not use background music in the current release candidate.

Will the game contain ads?

The planned free release includes banner advertising on eligible non-gameplay screens and an optional rewarded ad for one additional continue. Testing builds may use Google test ads.

When will Paws Above be available?

The Android game is currently being prepared for Google Play internal testing.

Contact support

Include the app version, Android version and a brief description of what happened. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information.
