Privacy Policy

How Paws Above handles information in the Android game.

Pre-release status: This policy describes the current testing build. Production AdMob identifiers and regional consent controls are still being completed and will be reviewed before broader testing or public release.

Effective date: 6 August 2026

Developer: Manan Shah

Privacy contact: mobileapps.manan@gmail.com

1. Overview

Paws Above is an Android endless-runner game. It does not require an account and does not provide developer-operated cloud storage or synchronisation.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of game information locally, including:

  • your saved high scores;
  • whether the first-run tutorial has been completed;
  • your sound-effects volume and mute preference.

This information is used only to provide the game, remember your preferences and show your results. Manan does not receive this local game information automatically.

3. Advertising

Paws Above uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The current design includes banner advertising on eligible non-gameplay screens and an optional rewarded advertisement that may offer one additional continue after a run ends.

During development or internal testing, Google test advertising identifiers may be used. Live advertising identifiers and regional consent controls will be finalised before broader testing or public release.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as an IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location.

Paws Above does not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising mediation or a developer-created user profile in the current configuration.

Learn more in Google's partner-sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Information Paws Above does not require

The game does not require your name, email address, phone number, account credentials or precise location. It does not request access to contacts, the camera or the microphone for normal gameplay.

5. Data sharing

Local scores and preferences are not shared with Manan unless you deliberately include information in a support message. Google and its advertising services may process information as described in Section 3 and in Google's own policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on the device until Android app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Support emails remain with the email service until deleted in accordance with ordinary support and recordkeeping needs.

Paws Above has no user accounts, so there is no separate account-deletion process.

7. Security

Local game information is stored within Android's app storage. Google states that information collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Audience and children's privacy

Paws Above is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not designed or marketed as a children's app. The game does not ask users to provide their age, name or contact details.

If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support request, contact mobileapps.manan@gmail.com.

9. Your choices

  • You can play without creating an account.
  • You can adjust or mute sound effects in Settings.
  • You can decline an optional rewarded-ad offer and end the run normally.
  • You can clear local game information through Android app-storage controls or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can manage or reset the Android advertising identifier through Android settings where available.
  • You can contact Manan about information you deliberately sent for support.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when advertising, consent configuration, app features or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to mobileapps.manan@gmail.com.
