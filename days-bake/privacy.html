Privacy policy

Day's Bake is designed as an offline-first bakery date board. Most information you enter remains on your device.

1. Overview

This privacy policy explains how Day's Bake handles information when you use the Android application. Day's Bake does not require an account and does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronisation.

2. Information stored on your device

The app can store bakery-related information locally, including:

  • product and group names;
  • shelf-life and reminder settings;
  • tracked bakery items, dates and optional quantities;
  • operational time-zone and app settings;
  • local audit history used to keep quantity and item changes reliable.

This information is used to provide the app's date-board, tracking, calendar and backup features.

3. Backups and file access

You may choose to export a backup file or import a backup through Android's system file picker. Day's Bake does not automatically upload these files. You choose the location and provider used to store or retrieve them.

4. Advertising and consent

The planned production version of Day's Bake uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required. Depending on your region, device settings and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, diagnostics and ad interactions.

Before release, confirm this section against the final SDK configuration and the declarations shown in Google AdMob and Google Play.

For information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, visit Google's partner-sites policy.

5. Information we do not require

Day's Bake does not require a user account, name, phone number or bakery employee identifier. The app is not intended to collect sensitive personal details through its normal bakery-tracking fields.

6. Data sharing

Local bakery records are not shared with the developer unless you deliberately send information for support. Advertising and consent providers may process information as described above and in their own policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the device until you remove it through the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them from that location.

8. Security

Day's Bake uses Android platform storage and validates imported backup files before applying them. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Day's Bake is an operational bakery tool and is not designed for children. [CONFIRM FINAL TARGET-AUDIENCE WORDING]

10. Your choices

  • You may use the app without creating an account.
  • You control whether and where backup files are exported.
  • Where required, privacy choices for advertising can be reviewed through the app's Settings screen.
  • You can remove local information by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
