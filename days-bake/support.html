Help and contact

Support

Day's Bake is built to be small, clear and dependable. These notes cover the most common questions.

Does Day's Bake require an account?

No. The app is designed to work without sign-in.

Where is my bakery data stored?

Product templates, tracked items, history and settings are stored locally on the Android device.

Can I move my data to another device?

You can export a validated backup file and import it through Android's system file picker.

Why am I seeing an advertisement?

Day's Bake is supported by one restrained banner advertisement. Consent and privacy options are shown where required.

Where can I download Day's Bake?

Day's Bake is available from Google Play.

Contact support

Include the app version, Android version and a brief description of what happened. Do not send passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal details.
